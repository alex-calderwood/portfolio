> "Il n'y a pas de hors-texte." ("There is nothing outside the text.")
>
> — Jacques Derrida, Of Grammatology

Nothing But is an interface to a tiny predictive text model. Designed to both help explain how AI models generate text, and to create a predictive-keyboard like way to craft language.
