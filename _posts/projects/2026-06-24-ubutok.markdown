UbuWeb is a vast, independent archive of avant-garde art, film, sound, and text maintained since 1996 by Kenneth Goldsmith. UbuWeb hosts thousands of works: films, sound poetry, concrete poetry, outsider music, early net art, and more. UbuTok surfaces that material at random, one piece at a time.

UbuTok is a dislocacated scroll through that material, started as something between some kind of joke about attention and the avant garde, and a desire to have some means of randomly pulling up the kind of avant garde content that UbuWeb hosts.

All content is served directly from UbuWeb. This is an unofficial interface created by Alex.
